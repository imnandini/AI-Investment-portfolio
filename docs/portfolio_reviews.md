# Saved portfolio assessments

Start a review in **Portfolio reviews**. No uploaded company reports are required. Version 2 combines fresh available daily prices, all saved holdings, current unrealized price returns, concentration, known sector metadata and bounded public-news checks. Saved version 1 reports remain readable as historical snapshots; start a new review to use the news-based format. Existing reports are not rewritten or deleted by this change.

Every holding is calculated. Up to 20 listed stocks/ETFs are selected for news, ranked by complete base-currency market value when available, otherwise by known base-currency recorded cost. Unknown/foreign-currency values sort last. The coverage panel states the total/eligible/selected/checked counts, holdings with and without headlines, unreviewed positions, excluded asset types, distinct article links and share of security value/cost checked. A 100-holding portfolio does not require 100 uploads: it receives calculations for 100 positions and a clearly labeled news sample. Remaining holdings can be researched through their individual news briefs; they are not silently cleared of risk.

The graph verifies ownership, refreshes prices, snapshots instrument names and calculations, searches optional documents sequentially, and fetches public company-name news with four concurrent requests and a 25-second news budget. It does not send portfolio quantities, costs, balances or the user's question to news providers. Pending checks and source failures remain visible. Cached feeds keep their original check time. News sources show publisher, publication date, check date and original links; identifiers are unique within the saved report, and schema validation rejects unmatched citations.

The assessment explains the largest holding by company/fund name and its weight. Where market weights are complete, a 10% single-holding price-move scenario quantifies the corresponding basket change while holding other prices/quantities fixed. Cost weights are described as purchase-capital exposure and are never used as current market sensitivity. Findings also describe known sector concentration, missing sector data, available return contributors, and headline topics with why they may matter and specific follow-up checks. ETFs/funds are labeled and do not receive operating-company earnings signals; underlying holdings, fund overlap and look-through sector exposure remain unknown unless separately supplied.

Headline signals use the existing conservative topic rules, including negation/forecast exclusions. They do not verify full articles, business fundamentals, valuation, market expectations or suitability. The review is a rules-based source-linked research view, not unrestricted generative analysis or a buy/sell rating. The 25% single-name and 40% sector levels are research prompts, not prescribed limits. Returns exclude dividends, realized trades, fees and taxes and are not annualized.

`partial` now means a material coverage gap, such as incomplete valuation/cost inputs, missing sector/look-through information, unavailable company news, unchecked listed holdings or unsupported asset types. Missing optional uploads and general research caveats do not by themselves force `partial`. Full source coverage likewise does not establish investment safety. Old saved reviews keep their original status.

`POST /portfolios/{id}/reviews` saves `portfolio-review-v2`. Existing authenticated history/read/delete endpoints are unchanged. Deleting a selected saved review remains an explicit user action.
